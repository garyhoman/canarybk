Location

Right in the heart of Crown Heights.

The Canary is located at 789 Franklin Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11238, right in the heart of Crown Heights and within easy reach of some of Brooklyn’s biggest attractions - being a stones throw away from Brooklyn Museum, Brooklyn Botanical Gardens & Prospect Park, with Barclays Center located a 10 minute Subway ride away.

Whether you’re looking for a pint and some food after the museum, drinks before a show or game, or somewhere to settle in after a day exploring Brooklyn, we’re right around the corner.

Explore Brooklyn. Then Come Have A Pint.

We’re steps from the Franklin Ave–Medgar Evers College subway station, served by the 2, 3, 4 & 5 trains, with the S shuttle at Botanic Garden just a few minutes away.
